Airbnb & Booking.com sync

Your platform calendars, kept in step.

Paste each listing’s calendar link once. Every hour CoastStay imports the platform’s bookings as blocked dates, so a direct guest can never take a night Airbnb has already sold.

  • Overlaps are flagged for you to decide; nothing is changed behind your back
  • Private calendar links stay hidden, even from your staff
  • Clear status for each feed: last synced, any problems
